## Users

## Product Purpose

## Capabilities and Constraints
Next.js and real FalkorDB remain mandatory. Delay, recovery, source inspection, graph-grounded analysis, and recorded approvals must work. Data is synthetic. No external messages are sent. Free Render hosting is authorized and ephemeral. Operator access must remain protected. The redesign should make the working demo accessible without disclosing an operator credential.

## Evidence on Hand
Repository source, synthetic seed, existing browser tests, published demo recording, and prior live deployment. No customer claims or real ERP integrations.

## Open Decisions
Public demo session structure and visual direction are implementation choices for this repair; do not imply enterprise availability or an LLM integration.
